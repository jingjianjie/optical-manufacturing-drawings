# Mechanical recipe and archive schema

Units: millimetres; bevel_angle: degrees. JSON top-level `parts` list. Each part has unique `name`, `front`, `back`, `radius`, `front_face`, `back_face`; derived center_thickness/edge_thickness/min_thickness are recalculated from API+profile during intake. The current substrate has two adjacent LDE surfaces. `front/back` and face.surface are **LDE index minus one**; array index zero corresponds to LDE1, not OBJECT. Name mapping is explicit in drawing_manifest; legacy `_positive/_negative` names are also understood.

Each face: surface, curve_end (radial sphere endpoint), transition (radial straight interpolation width), plateau (axial sag of outer flat relative to vertex), outer_bevel (radial width), bevel_angle, inward (+1 or −1 axial material-removal direction). sphere_sag is the signed parent curve. Transition goes from sphere_sag(curve_end) to plateau, then stays flat; outer bevel starts at radius−outer_bevel and adds inward·tan(angle)·radial_distance. All breakpoints lie within the actual substrate radius; outer bevel should not cut into the approved clear area. See profile_geometry.py for exact formulas.

Two parts sharing an interface can have different radius values and independent outside edges. Their optical curve in the cemented contact region agrees. Mechanical shape parameters are not a second optical prescription. A public example generator shows schema use without private source files.

`drawctl init` archive input: data/api_surfaces.json, mechanical.json, material_optics.json, drawing_manifest.json; source/*_source.zos; review/main_direct_api.json with matching source hash and surface fields. native/ and dxf/90_appendix*.dxf supply evidence/attachments. Empty evidence is allowed during development, not a claim of acceptance. The synthetic fixture carries SYNTHETIC_EXAMPLE.json, uses source/synthetic_source.json instead of a fake ZOS, and cannot be formally released.

Required surface record fields: surface, radius (null for plane), thickness, material, ca, mechanical, chip, stop, frame (successful GetGlobalMatrix tuple). Optional catalog/nd/vd enrich labels. material_optics.efl_d_mm maps explicit efl_key to independent part/group EFL. Manifest rows contain id, parts names, efl_key and optional layout anchors. project.json has relative copied source/snapshot/template/font/evidence paths, chosen profile, page overrides and review scope; hashes bind cached results.
